# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the OpenTrace community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for different viewpoints,
and taking responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal
attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private information, or conduct inappropriate in
a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports
should be made privately through the repository's security contact or maintainer contact shown
on GitHub. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.
